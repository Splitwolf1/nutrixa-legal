Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

THE SHORT VERSION
  • Your log is saved on your phone first, then backed up to your account.
  • Meal photos and voice clips go to our AI service only to read the meal.
  • Apple Health is shared only if you turn it on.
  • No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data.
  • Delete everything any time: Profile › Account.

Nutrixa is a calorie and nutrition tracker. This policy explains what data the app uses, where it goes and the choices you have. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Information We Collect

What you give us:

• Account: your email and name, from Apple, Google or email sign-in
• Profile: age, sex, height, weight, activity level, goals, diet and allergies
• Your log: meals, calories, macros, water and weight
• Meal photos, voice clips and text you send to AI logging
• Apple Health, only if you turn it on: Nutrixa reads active energy, height and weight, and writes the food, water and weight you log

Collected automatically:

• App usage events (screens opened, features used) and basic device info such as model, system version and language
• A push token, if you allow notifications
• Whether you have Nutrixa Premium

How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

• Run the app: save and back up your log, work out your calorie and macro targets, show your progress
• Read meals from your photos, voice clips and text with AI
• Send the reminders you choose
• Unlock and restore Premium
• See which features work, so we can improve the app
• Answer you when you contact support

We never use your data for advertising, and Apple Health data is never used for marketing or shared for that purpose.

Data Storage and Security

• Your log is saved on your phone first, then backed up to your Nutrixa account so it moves with you to a new phone
• Your account and backups are kept on servers we run ourselves (Appwrite)
• Usage events go to an analytics server we run ourselves (Rybbit), not to an advertising or analytics company
• Everything sent between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS)
• Only the Nutrixa team can access these servers

Who We Share Data With

We never sell your data. We share it only with the services that make the app work, and only what each one needs:

• OpenAI: meal photos, voice clips and meal text, sent through our server when you use AI logging, so the meal can be read. OpenAI does not use this data to train its models.
• RevenueCat: your app user ID and purchase history, to manage Premium
• Apple and Google: to sign you in and to handle App Store and Google Play payments
• Expo: your push token, to deliver notifications
• USDA FoodData Central: the words you search for, with no personal data

We may also share data when the law requires it, or when you ask us to. We do not track you: your data is never combined with other companies' data for advertising and never shared with data brokers.

Your Rights

• See and export your data: Profile › Account › Export my data
• Change your profile and log at any time in the app
• Delete your account and all its data: Profile › Account › Delete account
• Stop Apple Health access in the Health app, and notifications in Settings
• Ask us anything about your data at support@brianlite.in

If you live in the EU, the UK or California, you have these rights under the GDPR or the CCPA, and you can also contact your local data protection authority.

Data Retention

• We keep your data while your account is open
• When you delete your account, its data is removed from our servers and the app clears it from your phone
• Usage events are kept only to see trends over time
• Nutrixa is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data
